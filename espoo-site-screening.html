What the data says

An urban-edge site in Espoon Keskus (postal area 02770): ~800 people in the on-site 1 km cell but ~101,000 within 5 km, on land Copernicus classes as dense urban fabric beside forest and existing industrial/commercial land. A high-voltage line is ~1.1 km away (favorable proximity — subject to capacity & permitting). No mapped flood hazard (nearest 1/1000-yr zone 5.4 km off); the main environmental flags are a class-1 groundwater area 2.6 km away and a Natura 2000 SAC 4.6 km off — neither at the site, but both worth watching.
